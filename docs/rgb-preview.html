My recommendation

Your caps are PBT, non-shine-through, and the LEDs are south-facing — so per-key effects read as a soft underglow halo, never lit legends. That means:

1. A busy per-key animation is mostly wasted; a calm Reactive Heatmap gives a warm bloom as you type (your old vibe) without noise.
